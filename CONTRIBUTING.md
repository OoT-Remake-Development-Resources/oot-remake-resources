# Contributing to OoT Remake Development Resources

Thank you for contributing to OoT Remake Development Resources! This organization aims to create a collaborative, accessible, and reliable collection of research, documentation, tools, and resources related to the Ocarina of Time remake.

Contributions of research, documentation, testing, references, tools, corrections, and ideas are welcome.

## 1. Ways to Contribute

You can contribute in several ways:

- **Research:** Investigate game mechanics, systems, technical structures, behavior, or other unknowns.
- **Documentation:** Write guides, explain findings, organize information, or improve existing documentation.
- **Testing and verification:** Reproduce findings, test assumptions, and identify inconsistencies.
- **Resources:** Share relevant tools, repositories, references, and documentation.
- **Mapping:** Contribute maps, annotations, coordinates, or spatial information.
- **Development:** Contribute tools, scripts, applications, or other projects.
- **Corrections:** Identify inaccurate, outdated, incomplete, or misleading information.

## 2. Submitting Contributions

Contributions may be submitted through GitHub Issues, Discussions, or Pull Requests, depending on the nature of the contribution.

- **Issues:** Use Issues to report problems, propose tasks, request documentation changes, or coordinate work.
- **Discussions:** Use Discussions for questions, ideas, general research conversations, and proposals that aren't yet defined tasks.
- **Pull Requests:** Use Pull Requests to submit changes to documentation, data, code, or other repository contents.

If you're unsure where something belongs, feel free to open a Discussion.

## 3. Research Documentation Guidelines

Research contributions should aim to be clear, reproducible, and transparent.

When applicable, document:

- **Objective:** What question, behavior, system, or feature is being investigated?
- **Method:** How was the finding obtained or tested?
- **Observation:** What was directly observed?
- **Conclusion:** What does the observation suggest or establish?
- **Environment:** Game version, build, configuration, tools, or other relevant conditions.
- **Evidence:** Screenshots, recordings, logs, references, test results, or other supporting material.
- **Limitations:** Conditions that may affect the reliability or applicability of the finding.

Not every contribution requires every field. Use judgment based on the nature of the research.

Distinguish direct observations from interpretations, hypotheses, and assumptions. Findings that haven't been independently verified should be identified as such.

## 4. Verification and Accuracy

Contributors are encouraged to independently verify findings whenever practical.

Documentation should distinguish between:

- **Verified:** Reproduced or confirmed through sufficiently reliable evidence.
- **Partially verified:** Confirmed under specific conditions, but not fully tested.
- **Unverified:** Reported observation or hypothesis that hasn't yet been confirmed.
- **Disputed:** Conflicting evidence or interpretations exist.

Verification status may be updated as additional evidence becomes available.

Corrections and contradictory findings are welcome. Research should be evaluated based on its evidence, not the contributor's identity or experience.

## 5. Organization and Formatting

Please:

- Place contributions in the appropriate repository and directory.
- Follow the existing documentation structure and formatting conventions where practical.
- Use descriptive filenames and headings.
- Avoid unnecessarily duplicating information that already exists.
- Link to existing documentation and related projects when appropriate.
- Update indexes or references when adding new documentation.

If a contribution doesn't fit the existing structure, propose a new section or directory rather than forcing it into an unrelated category.

## 6. Sources and Attribution

Contributors should identify and credit sources used in their contributions.

- Link to original sources whenever possible.
- Credit other contributors for their work and discoveries.
- Identify third-party material and its applicable license or usage restrictions.
- Avoid presenting someone else's work as your own.
- Do not submit material that you don't have permission to redistribute.

Whenever possible, link to the original resource rather than duplicating it.

## 7. Licensing

Unless otherwise indicated, original documentation and other eligible non-code contributions to this repository are made available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit the material and agree to its distribution under the applicable repository license.

Code, third-party material, and other content may be subject to separate licensing terms.

## 8. Community Expectations

Contributors are expected to:

- Treat other contributors respectfully.
- Provide constructive criticism and feedback.
- Be open to corrections and alternative interpretations.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory behavior.
- Focus disagreements on evidence, methodology, and ideas.
- Respect the privacy and safety of other community members.

## 9. Sensitive and Restricted Material

Do not submit:

- Personal information that shouldn't be publicly disclosed.
- Credentials, authentication tokens, or private account information.
- Copyrighted or proprietary material that you aren't authorized to redistribute.
- Material that violates the applicable laws, platform rules, or repository policies.

Potentially sensitive security findings should be responsibly disclosed rather than publicly posted before appropriate review.

## 10. Review Process

Contributions may be reviewed for accuracy, clarity, organization, licensing, relevance, and compliance with these guidelines.

Maintainers may request revisions, additional evidence, clarification, or changes to formatting before accepting a contribution.

Contributions may be declined if they are outside the scope of the organization, unsupported by adequate evidence, improperly sourced, or otherwise inconsistent with repository policies.

Thank you for helping make OoT Remake Development Resources a useful and collaborative community resource!

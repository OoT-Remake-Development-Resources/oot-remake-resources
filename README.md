# OoT Remake Development Resources

A collaborative hub for research, documentation, tools, modding, mapping, and development projects related to the Ocarina of Time remake.

This organization aims to consolidate community knowledge, make technical research more accessible, encourage collaboration between independent projects, and provide a shared foundation for future development.

## Areas of Research and Development

- **Technical Research** – Investigating game systems, architecture, data structures, behavior, and technical characteristics.
- **Modding** – Tools, techniques, documentation, mods, and compatibility.
- **Randomizers** – Randomizer development, logic research, testing, and shared resources.
- **Mapping** – Maps, spatial data, annotations, and exploration.
- **Tools** – Utilities and development tools for the remake.
- **Documentation** – Community-maintained knowledge and references.

## Randomizer Research

The [OoT Remake Randomizer Research](https://github.com/OoT-Remake-Development-Resources/oot-remake-randomizer-research) repository serves as a shared workspace for randomizer-related research, documentation, logic investigations, testing, and resources.

This repository is intended to facilitate collaboration and knowledge-sharing between independent randomizer projects.


## Emulation & Recompilation Research

The [OoT Remake Emulation & Recompilation Research](https://github.com/vgdevz/oot-remake-emulation-and-recomp-research) repository serves as a shared workspace for research, documentation, resources, and development efforts related to emulation and recompilation of the Ocarina of Time remake.
